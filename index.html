Healthcare Analytics Project: Excel, SQL, Python and Power BI

An end-to-end healthcare analytics project built on a synthetic dataset of 16,625 patient encounters, to show how I move from raw data to KPIs and dashboards.

Key insights

  • Emergency and Internal Medicine carry the volume. Emergency Medicine had 2,675 encounters (370 admissions, 13.83% admission rate) and Internal Medicine 2,468 (360 admissions, 14.59%). Together they were about 30.9% of all encounters.
  • Oncology has the highest admission rate: 17.12%, ahead of Cardiology (16.03%) and Orthopedics (15.97%), even though it is not a high-volume department.
  • Admission demand peaks in December: 16.70% (235 admissions from 1,407 encounters) against a low of 12.00% in October.
  • Imaging is the most frequent procedure category: 2,118 procedure records.

Recommendations

  • Improve patient flow in Emergency and Internal Medicine: track arrival-to-triage and admission-decision times and set discharge-planning targets.
  • Plan for December demand: review seasonal trends, keep staffing flexible and schedule elective procedures with the peak in mind.

Deliverables: enriched datasets, an Excel workbook with a KPI dashboard and pivot analysis, SQL views and analytical queries, a Python analysis script with charts, an interactive HTML dashboard, and a Power BI data model with DAX measures and theme.
